# Canon log

Every lore detail and setting system in the game is checked here against the LANCER core book
(page numbers from the free version), and where the book is thin, against other official
Massif material and Lancer Tactics. Rules mechanics are tracked in [RULES_TODO.md](RULES_TODO.md).
Ideas that aren't canon are parked in DESIGN.md, "Parked: your own story additions", and
aren't built until the user turns them on.

Status: **Canon** = stated in an official source. **Consistent** = not stated, but fits
what the book says and contradicts nothing. **Not canon** = contradicts or goes beyond the
book in a way that changes the setting; parked.

## When canon allows more than one answer

Sometimes the book allows several readings or choices (e.g. keep your mech and take it with
you, or reprint one wherever you go: both are canon, Core p.31, p.82). Research each canon
option, and support all of them as player choices instead of picking one.

| Question | Canon options | How the game handles it |
|---|---|---|
| Carry your mech or reprint it? | Both: printers are everywhere and Full Repair prints any licensed mech; nothing forbids keeping one (p.31, p.82) | Your mech travels with the squad by default; reprint at a printer when destroyed or to change builds |
