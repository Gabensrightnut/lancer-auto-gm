# Ability report: tricky interactions, checked against the full core book

For each ability whose rules are tricky to play in a video game (illusions, attaching, teleports
and swaps, terrain changes, taking over units, turn tricks, copies, Hidden and Invisible, "the
GM decides", effects across scenes, persistent deployables, knowledge only one side has), this
says which of three groups it's in:

- **(a) Done exactly as the book says.**
- **(b) Possible, but it needs a choice from you** ("user ruling" once decided) (a "the GM decides" or narrative effect
  turned into something concrete). Each has a proposed default, used until you decide, marked
  PROVISIONAL in the code.
- **(c) Not possible as written in a video game.** Each has the closest faithful alternative.

Sorted by frame or licence. Batch 1 (more batches follow as the audit goes on). Book pages are
the full core book's.

## GMS (everyone)

| Ability | Group | What the game does |
|---|---|---|
| Personalizations (+1 on a check "if the GM agrees it helps") | (b) user ruling | When you build the mech, name the one skill trigger your personalization fits; you get +1 on checks with that trigger, never on others. |
| Reinforced Cabling: secure yourself to a wall or ceiling | (b) user ruling | You can walk onto and mount yourself on the side of a building (vertical walls), or onto ceilings when inside. While secured you move along that surface, can't fall or be knocked off, and fight from there with the book's cover and line of sight from your new position. (Being built.) |
| Portable Bunker | (a) | An object with 40 HP and Evasion 5; attack it as the bunker. |
| Sleeping Bag | (a) | No rules effect in the book (a comfort item); nothing to do in battle. |
| Pilot weapons Thrown, Signature weapons | (a) / in progress | Signature damage type is set when made. Thrown weapons landing next to the target and being picked up is being built now. |

## HORUS

| Ability | Group | What the game does |
|---|---|---|
| Goblin: Symbiosis (core) | (a) | Attaches to a larger, willing, adjacent ally: shares its space and moves with it, has hard cover, still targetable; takes the host's heat and conditions; the host uses its Systems, E-Defense and Tech Attack; from the next round it acts during the host's turn (two quick or one full action), can't move or Overcharge. |
| Goblin: Autopod | (a) | Fires only through its own reaction (Autonomous Assault), never by hand. |
| H0R_OS II: False Idol | (a) | A decoy of you or an ally (the book: yourself or an ally, the same Size; it can't copy enemies or terrain). Enemies who see it make a Systems save before acting against the real one; on a failure they can't target the real one until the end of their next turn. It has Evasion 10, E-Defense 10, 1 HP and vanishes when it takes damage or heat, or at the end of the scene. Enemies see it as the real mech (their view and their AI); your side sees it marked as an illusion. |
| H0R_OS II: seeing through the idol | (b) user ruling | The book only says a failed save fools you. A successful save lets that whole side see it for what it is (it stops looking real to them). |
| OSIRIS-Class NHP: the First Gate (you control the target's next standard move) | (b) user ruling | You move the target right away, up to its full Speed, by normal movement rules; the move provokes reactions as usual (Overwatch, engagement). (Being built.) |
| SCYLLA-Class NHP: second trigger (a hostile attacks or uses an object you chose) | (b) user ruling | When you activate it you pick the object; the reaction is then offered when a hostile uses it. |
| Interdiction Field: only characters you choose can teleport inside | (b) user ruling | You name the allowed characters when you activate it (default: your own side). |
| Repulser Field: you're immune to the mines it sets off | (b) user ruling | While you have it, you're immune to all mines, not only the ones the field sets off. (Being built.) |
| Lotus Projector: shows you HP, Evasion, E-Defense and heat of characters in its field | (b) user ruling | Your side gets those numbers in its view for characters in the field (one side's knowledge). |
